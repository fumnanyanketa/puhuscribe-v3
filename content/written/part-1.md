# Part 1. First words (stops 1 to 6)

Written by Claude on 1 November 2026 for the owner's 36-stop A1 road (`docs/curriculum-a1.md`). Not yet run through the automatic checks or the native reviewer. Maria, a newcomer from Brazil, is the learner's companion through the road.

Stops 1 to 3 already have two drafted dialogues each in `content/drafts/` (A new colleague, At a language café, Ordering coffee at the counter, Paying for coffee, Directions in the grocery store, Asking the staff). This file adds their third dialogue.

## Stop 1. Meeting people

**I can:** say who I am and where I'm from, and ask the same.

### 1.3 A new neighbour

Maria meets her neighbour Leena in the stairwell.
