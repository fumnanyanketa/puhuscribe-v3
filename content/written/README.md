# Hand-written dialogues for the A1 road

Every dialogue of the 36-stop A1 road (`docs/curriculum-a1.md`), written by Claude in working sessions on 1 November 2026 at the owner's request, instead of by the pipeline's paid AI calls.

- `part-1.md` to `part-6.md`: 102 dialogues, 3 for each stop, 8 lines each, every line in spoken Finnish, written Finnish and English.
- Stops 1 to 3 also have two earlier dialogues each in `content/drafts/`, made by the pipeline. With those, the road has 108 dialogues.
- The fourth lesson of each stop is a review of that stop's words and is made by the app, not written here.

Status: not yet through the automatic checks (Voikko and the word frequency list run on GitHub) and not yet seen by the native reviewer. The owner decided to launch before native review; the reviewer's fixes replace lines after launch.

Next step: the content pipeline needs a small change to read these tables, run its checks, make the voices and publish them.
