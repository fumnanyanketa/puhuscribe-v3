# Language islands: one personal line per stop

Draft for the owner's approval (written 3 November 2026). Each stop gives the learner one sentence about their own life. `{gap}` is filled from that stop's list, never typed freely, so every Finnish word the learner sees has been checked. Names are the one exception: a name is shown as typed and never inflected.

Each line has spoken Finnish, written Finnish and English. Before the build, the importer checks every word against Voikko and against the forms the learner met in that stop and earlier; any word not yet met is listed for a fix.

Three stops have no personal line on purpose: At the pharmacy, At the health centre (health is sensitive data) and Problems and returns. Nothing asks about religion, money, health or an address. The island ends with a question back: "Entä sä?" / "Entä sinä?" (And you?)

## How the island shows

The road card "Your island" lists the learner's lines in spoken Finnish, each with a play button, then "Entä sä?". A Record button lets the learner say the whole island and play it back (audio stays on the phone, as Say it does today). The written lines show under each spoken line, like the lessons. Every line is voiced ahead of time in Selma's voice, once per choice in its list, so the app never sends anything about a learner to a voice service. The name is the exception: "Mä oon {name}." has no recorded voice, and the learner says it themselves.
