# Stop pictures

`public/stops/01.svg` to `36.svg` are the pictures of the 36 stops of the A1 road, one per stop, shown on the road signs and on the stop screen (fast route road-stops, approved by the owner on 5 November 2026).

- **Source.** Free illustrations from unDraw by Katerina Limpitsouni, https://undraw.co. The unDraw file used for each stop is listed in the owner's picks (`design-drafts/stop-pictures/picks.md` in the private working copy).
- **Licence.** The unDraw licence, https://undraw.co/license: free for personal and commercial use, no attribution needed. It does not allow building a competing illustration library from them.
- **Changes.** Recoloured to the app's mint and dark ink, background shapes removed, cropped tight, then shrunk with svgo 3.3.2 (one decimal place, viewBox kept, width and height removed).
