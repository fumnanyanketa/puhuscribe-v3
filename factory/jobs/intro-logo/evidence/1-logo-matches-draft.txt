The outlined logo against the approved drawing (design-drafts/wordmark/w4-refined.html, "After" column).

Method: the drawing was opened in headless Chromium with the self-hosted Poppins ExtraBold (the drawing
asks Google Fonts for the same font). Its fell A, the letter positions of "leksı" and its bubble were read
from the page. The outlined logo (src/components/wordmark-paths.ts) was drawn in the same frame at the same
size (860 px wide, about 5 times the 167 unit width) and both were compared pixel by pixel.

Result: 181 pixels differ by more than a faint anti-aliasing shade, out of 89,712 ink pixels (0.2 percent),
all along the letters' edges. 1-logo-difference.png shows them in red over a faint copy of the drawing.

Files: 1-logo-draft-w4-after.png (the drawing), 1-logo-outlined.png (the outlines), 1-logo-difference.png.
