A. Calm reader with audio

Feel. Like reading along with a podcast. A cool light background, dark ink text, one deep blue for the controls, and the spoken word lit up.

For learners. The easiest to read on a phone on the bus, light on older phones, and calm for people already under pressure.

Cost and risk. €0. It can look plain unless the spacing and type are done with real care.

Borrows from. Apple Podcasts transcripts, Google's dictation app, Waking Up, Oura, Speak's lesson layout.

B. Helsinki public-service clarity

Feel. Crisp and official. Pure white, black outlines, square corners, a strong blue for every button, a yellow highlight.

For learners. The most accessible and it feels local. It may also feel like the Migri and Kela forms that cause the pressure in the first place.

Cost and risk. €0. It can read as a city service more than a product people choose.

Borrows from. The Helsinki Design System, Lingvis.

C. One flat colour for each loop step, with photos

Feel. Bold and recognisable. Each step of the loop owns a colour (blue here for Hear it), with a real photo of the situation at the top.

For learners. Always clear which step they are on, and the situation feels real. Busier on a small screen.

Cost and risk. Photos cost time or money, light and dark contrast is harder per colour, and Finnish text on signs in photos would need your reviewer.

Borrows from. NYT Games, Babbel's rebrand.

All three use your fonts: Geist for headings, Lexend for the Finnish and body text, Geist Mono for small labels. The Finnish lines are from your Test 2 voice lines and still wait for your reviewer's check.

How the colours are built, in any direction

Also checked and left out: Senthora (senthora.ai), whose look is a dark purple glow with gradient headline text, both on your banned list. Only its idea of words lighting up as you read aloud carries over, and Apple's transcripts show it better. Pinterest's language-app pins were mostly gamified kits and AI-made mockups.

How to choose

Tell Claude A, B or C, or describe a mix, such as A with C's colour for each step. Nothing is built until you pick. After that, one real sample screen is made for you to see on your phone before the full design system.
