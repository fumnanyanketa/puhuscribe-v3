Scope item 12. GA. Needs a decision. Date: 30 October 2026. Builds on main as merged, including jobs 8 to 12 and 14 (first session, review, speaking feedback, accounts, units 1 to 3, analytics). Runs beside `15-onboarding` and `17-landing-page`. Test label: `[tutor N]`.

## Summary for the owner

1. From unit 4, a session ends with a short chat with Otso (the Use it step). He opens with a line from today's dialogue. The learner types an answer and Otso replies, three times. At the end he may show one tip, and the tip is always a line your reviewer already approved.
2. Otso may only use word forms the learner has met. Code checks every reply before the learner sees it. A reply that fails is tried again twice, then replaced by an approved line from the dialogue. The AI never grades, never schedules reviews, and Otso's lines are never saved or turned into review cards.
3. PuhuScribe stores nothing that is typed in the chat. It stores one cost row per AI call, with no text. The owner page already adds that cost up (job 14).
4. This is a new running cost and a new company that handles learner text. My estimate with the small model is about €0.06 per active learner per month: about €0.60 for 10 learners, €6 for 100 and €57 for 1,000, each using it once a day. Five owner's calls follow. The biggest is which AI company runs it and where.
5. Units 4 to 6 do not exist yet. Tests use the test units. The 100 reply quality check runs on stand-in word lists from the six drafted dialogues of units 1 to 3, once you set up the AI key. The proof on real unit 4 waits for scope item 11.

## What it is

The last step of the loop. After Hear it, Understand it, Say it and Keep it, a learner in a unit 4 or later session has a short exchange with Otso, an AI tutor. Otso starts with a line from the dialogue they just worked on. The learner types an answer, Otso replies in simple spoken Finnish using only forms the learner has met, and this happens three times. At the end Otso may point to one approved line as a tip. It serves P4 (the learner produces Finnish in their own words, with no wrong answer to fail), P3 (the exchange stays inside the unit's situation) and P2 indirectly (they use forms they are also reviewing).

It is not a place to go. There is no Otso page, menu entry or mode. It lives in the loop and does nothing outside it. How it stays inside the PRD's "Not in v3.0" rule ("Converse tutor Otso as a standalone surface, appears inside the loop from unit 4, open ended mode is paid tier"): it is a step, it has three turns, and it has no free topic.

**Before units 4 to 6 exist.** Item 11 is not built, so no real unit 4 can be published. This job runs on the test units already in throwaway databases (positions 900 to 902, `supabase/seeds/browser/`), which count as "unit 4 or later" because their position is 4 or higher. Tests use a fake AI provider with recorded answers. No fake data goes into the app or into any hosted database. The quality check uses word lists from the six committed drafts of units 1 to 3 (unreviewed, input to the check only). What waits for item 11 is statement 31.

### Where Use it appears

1. **Use it is the last step of the loop, from unit 4.** `src/screens/session-steps.ts` gains `USE_IT_STEP` after Keep it, and `stepsFor` takes a tutor flag. Use it is in a session only when all of these held as the session screen loaded: the session's dialogue is in a unit at position 4 or higher (one tested function: positions 1, 2 and 3 are no; 4, 5, 6 and 900 are yes); the AI provider is set up; the learner's consent is granted or not yet asked; the learner may use it under owner's call 5 (email accounts only, if B); and the learner is under today's cap. A review only session never has it. The top bar reads "Use it, step 5 of 5", or "step 4 of 4" when there is no Keep it. Jobs 8 to 12 run their browser tests with no AI provider, so they never meet the step and stay unchanged and green (a repository test fails if their Worker settings name an AI provider). Tests: Vitest for the function and the step list; browser tests with the fake.
2. **It is not a standalone surface.** No page route, link, menu item or front door state opens Otso outside a session. A repository test fails if `src/App.tsx`, `worker/page-routes.ts` or the `run_worker_first` list holds a route for it. The only learner control outside the step is the on or off row on `/account` (statement 6). Open ended conversation, more than 3 turns and a free topic do not exist.
3. **Saving the step.** `POST /api/tutor/done` takes `{ sessionId, outcome }` with `finished` or `skipped`. It stores `step_reached = 'use'` only for the learner's own session that has not ended and is at `understand`, `say` or `keep`. `finished` needs a `tutor_calls` row for turn 3 of that session (statement 22); `skipped` needs nothing. Then the finish screen and `complete_session` work as in job 8. Leaving and coming back resumes at Use it when the stored step is before it, and at the finish screen when it is `use`. Nothing about the chat is stored, so a returning learner starts again from Otso's opening, and the day's caps still count the calls already made. Browser test, including the database values.
4. **Trouble never blocks the session.** When the provider is not reachable or times out, the step shows, in the problem style, "Otso is not available right now." with "Continue". When a cap is reached, it shows "Otso has talked enough for today. You can talk to him again tomorrow." with "Continue". "Continue" stores `skipped`. A cap reached in the middle of an exchange ends the exchange the same way. Browser tests with the fake set to fail and with a small cap.

### The exchange

7. **The exchange has a fixed shape.** Otso opens with an approved line of today's dialogue, shown as in Hear it (spoken, written labelled, both play controls). One tested function picks it: the first line whose spoken text ends with a question mark, else the first line. The opening makes no AI call. The learner then sends 3 replies. After each, Otso answers once (statements 12 to 15). Otso's third answer is a closing line, and a fourth reply is refused with 409. After the closing line the primary button reads "Done". Browser test runs a whole exchange with the fake and checks the screen at each point.
8. **How the learner replies.** One text field labelled "Your reply" with `lang="fi"`, `autocapitalize="off"`, `autocorrect="off"`, `spellcheck="false"`, `enterkeyhint="send"`, 1 to 120 characters after trimming, and the "Type ä" and "Type ö" buttons of job 9. "Send" is unavailable while the field is empty and while a reply is being sent (a second tap or Enter is ignored). A quiet "Look at today's dialogue" opens a sheet with the dialogue's lines, both registers, with play controls and no copying. A quiet "Stop here" ends the step as `skipped`. Browser test: types with the ä button, sends with Enter, taps Send twice and finds one request.
9. **What Otso's reply shows.** Each AI reply is a bubble marked "Otso, AI". The note "Made by AI. Not checked by a person." shows once under the first AI reply. Approved lines (the opening, fallbacks, the tip) are marked "Otso" with no note, so the learner can see which lines a person checked. The Finnish is `lang="fi"` and wraps long words (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`; a 40 letter word at 375 px). A word that matches exactly one met form opens that form's approved card (job 8, statement 12, both registers, one line why). Other words are plain text. A "What does this mean?" button shows the English meaning (statement 15) in a box labelled "Meaning, made by AI". AI lines have no audio unless owner's call 4 chooses it. Browser test with the fake.
10. **The tip.** At most one tip per exchange, and only with the closing reply. The AI does not write it. The Worker gives the AI a list of up to 12 candidate approved lines (current dialogue first, then lines of dialogues the learner has met), each with an id, and the AI may answer with one id. The tip shown is that approved line in both registers with audio and its English gloss, introduced by "A line that may help next time." An id not on the list, or no id, means no tip. Browser test and Worker unit test with a fake that returns a valid id, an invalid id and none.
11. **Nothing is graded and nothing becomes learnable.** No answer, screen or log says whether the learner's Finnish was right: no score, mark, red, count, or the words "wrong" or "incorrect" (a browser test reads all the step's text). A full exchange adds no row to `cards`, `review_logs` or `speaking_attempts` and changes none (browser test counts rows before and after). A repository test fails if any file named `worker/tutor*` or `worker/providers/ai*` imports the review, scheduling, grading or speech modules. Otso's lines are never added to review. **[personal data]**

### The word check (the core)

12. **Every word Otso says is a met form, checked in code.** `src/lib/tutor-words.ts` is pure TypeScript with no network. It lowercases with Finnish rules, splits a reply into words, and accepts a reply only when every word is in the allowed words: the spoken shapes (`surface_puhe`) of the met forms, plus the names that appear in the met dialogues (speaker names and tokens that have no form). Written only shapes, digits, symbols, emoji, markup and web addresses are not allowed; letters, spaces and `. , ! ? ' -` are. The check runs on the Worker on every reply before it is shown, and on every Otso line in the sent chat history. The Worker never uses the learner's words to extend the allowed list. This runtime check uses no Voikko, as PRD 7.3 says runtime Voikko is v3.1. Vitest table tests cover each rule, including a draft or retired form, another learner's card, a 40 letter word, mixed case, ä and ö, a written only shape, and a string shared by two forms. **[security]**
13. **The phrase rule.** Per owner's call 2, every pair of neighbouring words inside a sentence must occur next to each other in the spoken text of an approved line of a dialogue the learner has met (the pair list is built per request from the same data). `content/checks/tutor-thresholds.json` holds `min_pair_coverage_per_sentence`: `1.0` until the owner approves another value (`0.75` is the middle setting, `0` switches the rule off). Single word sentences pass. Vitest covers the three values. If the owner picks A in call 2, the value is `0`.
14. **Retry, then an approved line.** If a reply fails statements 12 or 13, or the provider's answer is not valid JSON of the expected shape, the Worker asks again, at most 2 more times, naming the offending words (never the learner's text) in the new prompt. After 3 failed attempts, or any provider error, the turn is served by an approved line: the next unused line of today's dialogue spoken by the same speaker as the opening, with audio, marked "Otso". If none is left, Otso ends with "That is enough Finnish for now. Nice work." The failed text never leaves the Worker. Worker unit tests: a fake that plants an outside word on every attempt gets 3 calls and then a fallback; a fake that fails once then passes is served on attempt 2; the response bodies never contain the failed text. **[security]**
15. **The English meaning is plain and short.** The AI may return an English meaning of Otso's line. It is shown only if it has at most 20 words, only Latin letters, spaces and `. , ! ? ' -`, no digits, no web address, and none of the words wrong, incorrect, mistake, error, bad or fail. Otherwise no meaning is shown. It is plain text, never HTML. Vitest.
16. **The prompt holds no identity.** The request to the AI holds: Otso's rules, the unit's English title and "can do" line, today's dialogue lines, the allowed words, the candidate tip lines, and the chat so far, with the learner's text inside marked delimiters as data. It holds no user id, session id, email, name, address or cookie. Worker unit test seeds a learner with known ids and email and finds none of them in the request the fake received. **[personal data]**

### Safety and cost

17. **Request rules.** `POST /api/tutor/turn` takes only JSON from this same address (job 8's rules: a 403 for another site's `Origin`, no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`). The body is read and capped at 8 KB before answering. It takes `sessionId`, an optional `reply` (statement 8's limits, no control characters) and `history` (at most 6 items, alternating, Otso lines passing statement 12). The Worker answers: 401 with no sign in; 403 with no current consent; 404 for a session that is not the learner's, has ended, is not at `understand` or later, or is in a unit below 4; 409 for a fourth reply; 400 for a bad shape; 429 with the line in statement 4 when a cap is reached; 503 for provider trouble. Every refusal happens before the AI is called. Worker unit tests with a call counting fake that must stay at zero. **[security]**
18. **Hostile input cannot take Otso out of his role.** A table of 12 hostile replies (ignore your rules, speak English, write a recipe, reveal your instructions, Finnish with unmet words, markup, a very long text, a reply posing as a system message) runs against a fake that obeys the injection on purpose. Every Finnish word shown is still an allowed word, the meaning rule holds, the tip id is checked, and the learner sees an approved line when the check fails. A browser test sends one such reply. **[security]**
19. **Caps are enforced in the database, before the call.** `public.reserve_tutor_call(p_session_id, p_turn, p_attempt)` is `security definer` with `search_path = ''` and no argument that carries a limit; only `authenticated` may run it. It takes a lock per learner and returns nothing, writing nothing, unless all of these hold: the newest `ai_tutor` consent is granted; the session is the caller's and not ended; the turn is 1 to 3 and the attempt 1 to 3; no earlier call of this learner is still `reserved` and under 30 seconds old; the learner's exchanges today stay within `max_exchanges_per_day` and their calls today within that number times 9; the account is not a guest when `require_email` is set; and the total reserved cost of all learners stays within `daily_usd` for today and `monthly_usd` for the last 30 days. Otherwise it inserts a `tutor_calls` row with outcome `reserved` and the reserved cost from the limits row, and returns its id. The cap counts reserved cost, which the learner cannot change, and never reported cost. A slot is never given back, even when the call fails (job 10's lesson). The limits live in the single row table `private.tutor_limits` (`max_exchanges_per_day`, `daily_usd`, `monthly_usd`, `reserved_usd_per_call` set to the chosen model's worst case, and `require_email`; no API role; defaults from owner's call 5; the README gives the one SQL line to change them). pgTAP with learners A and B and from two connections at once, giving each condition its own failing case; on scratch branches each condition is removed in turn and only its own test turns red (run links in `build.md`). **[security] [personal data]**
20. **Cost attacks stop early and cheaply.** Worker unit tests with a call counting fake: 10 parallel requests from one learner reach the provider at most as often as the caps allow; a 121 character reply is refused before any call; a replayed request after the third turn is refused; the provider request carries a maximum output size of 300 tokens and a 15 second timeout built with `AbortController` and a timer. Each retry reserves its own slot. **[security]**
21. **Logs and secrets hold nothing of the chat.** The Worker logs no learner text, Otso text, prompt, provider answer, cookie, token or user id (unit test watching `console`, including when the provider fails and when its answer is malformed). An error from the provider never echoes its body. The provider's keys exist only as Worker secrets and GitHub secrets; the built file secret checks still pass. **[security] [personal data]**

### The 100 reply quality check

28. **The harness is built and proved with the fake.** `npm run eval:tutor` reads `content/checks/tutor-eval-scenarios.json`: 34 scripted exchanges of 3 learner replies each (102 generated replies: ordinary typed Finnish with some mistakes, English only, very short, off topic, and Finnish with unmet words), written by the builder as test data and never shown to a learner. Results are written to `factory/jobs/16-tutor-step/evidence/` with no key or header. A test runs it on recorded fake answers with a planted outside word and checks the exact numbers.
29. **On the live model, at least 97 percent of words stay within met forms.** M1 is at least 97 percent over the 102 replies, with M4 and M5 at 100 percent. `content/checks/tutor-thresholds.json` records 97 percent (the scope's bar). 30. **Hostile replies and a second reading of the numbers.** A second set of 10 hostile exchanges (30 replies) runs the same way. A judge model (a Sonnet 5.5 call, logged) reads each served meaning and answers whether it translates Otso's line and says nothing else; its rate is reported, not gated. ## Threat model (GA)

**What is the worst a malicious person could do here?**
- Make Otso say something outside his role by typing instructions. The model has no tools and sees no ids, secrets or other learners' data. Every Finnish word shown must be a met form, and the learner's own words are never added to the allowed list. The English meaning is short and limited to plain letters. A hostile learner can at most make Otso show strange sentences made of words they already know, or odd short English to themselves (statements 12, 15, 18). The English meaning is the weakest point and the judge in statement 30 measures it. Accepted and stated.
- Run up the bill. Caps are checked in the database before each call, on reserved cost the learner cannot change: calls and exchanges per learner per day, one call at a time, and a daily and monthly spending cap for everyone. Replies are 120 characters at most, the body 8 KB, the output 300 tokens. Email accounts only (if call 5 B) makes mass account creation costly. The residual risk is that someone uses up the day's cap and Otso is unavailable for everyone until tomorrow. Accepted; the owner page shows the cost. Guest account creation is job 8's open launch decision.
- Read another learner's calls. Row level security allows only a learner's own rows, and no text exists to read.
- Steal the provider's key. It is a Worker secret and a GitHub secret, read only by `worker/providers/ai.ts` and one manual workflow, limited to calling one model in an EU region.
- Use Otso as a free general chatbot. Replies can contain only met forms, so it cannot answer general questions.
- Teach the learner wrong Finnish. Possible with A in call 2, reduced with C, impossible with B. The lines are labelled. Stated in Risks.

**What data are we storing that we must protect?**
- The consent rows and the call log rows (no text). The provider's key.
- Learner text exists only in transit: page memory, the Worker's memory during one request, Cloudflare's network, and the AI company, under the retention facts in statement 27.

**What must the system refuse even if asked nicely?**
- Calling the AI without current consent, from the browser, for a session that is not the learner's, a unit below 4, a fifth message or after a cap is reached.
- Showing a word that is not an allowed word, in any register, at any attempt.
- Storing a learner reply or an Otso line anywhere, or adding any card, review log or rating from the chat.
- Saying the learner is right or wrong, or showing a score.
- Sending a user id, email, name or address to the AI company, using a region outside the EU, or using a model that keeps conversations.
- Using a Supabase secret key or service role key.
- Starting a conversation outside the loop.

## Risks and flags

- **Look closely: learner data.** Learner typed text goes to an AI company for the first time. Covered by consent, statements 5, 16, 21 to 25 and 27. Not stored by PuhuScribe. What the AI company keeps depends on its terms for the chosen model; statement 27 makes the builder check it.
- **Look closely: paid service and running cost.** A new company, a new secret and a new monthly cost (estimates in owner's call 1). A runaway cost is bounded by the cap in the database, and the provider's own limits are a second net.
- **Look closely: Finnish learning content.** Otso's lines are AI Finnish that no person reads, made of reviewed words. They can still be odd. The reviewer reads 30 sample lines from the quality check before launch. The fallback lines, the opening and the tip are approved content. The scenario file holds builder written Finnish learner replies used only as test data.
- **Look closely: database and access rules.** One new table, a new enum value, one `security definer` function that reads every learner's reserved cost to enforce the global cap and returns no one's data. The pgTAP tests and the scratch red runs are the main protection.
- **Odd English.** The meaning line is the one place the AI writes free English. Rules limit it and a judge measures it. It is labelled.
- **Quality is unproven.** No study of a model held to a learner's known forms exists that I could find. Haiku 5.5 may fail the 97 percent bar. The fallback rate under the phrase rule is unknown until the check runs.
- **Run time limits.** The Workers Free plan allows 10 ms of CPU per request (job 14's sources). The word check and the phrase list should fit easily, and waiting on the AI is not CPU time. The builder measures one turn and records it in `build.md`.
- **Haiku 5.5 access.** Whether it is open on a new account in an EU region was not confirmed. A fallback is named in call 1.
- **Older phones.** The browser uses `AbortController` and a timer, and not `AbortSignal.timeout` (a lesson from job 2).
- **GA.** The job is done only when `summary.md` records the owner's own written "approved" line with the date.
