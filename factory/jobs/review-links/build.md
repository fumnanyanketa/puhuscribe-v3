# review-links (fast route)

The owner's calls of 24 November 2026: one press on the owner page sends a person who signed up at `meetaleksi.com/reviewers` their own private review link, with no Cloudflare step. The link opens a batch of 27 dialogues in road order, worked out from the live content; answers save as the reviewer goes; deleting the sign up turns the link off and deletes the answers. Spec: `factory/jobs/review-links/spec.md`. Flag: look closely (a secret link, a public page reachable only with it, new tables, an email to a person with no account).

## Round 1

### What was built

- **The database** (`supabase/migrations/20261124133711_review_links.sql`). Two tables in the private schema. `private.review_links`: one row per link sent, with the sign up (on delete cascade), the batch (1 to 4), the sha256 hash of the link's secret (never the secret), the time it was sent, and the answer count of the current minute. `private.review_answers`: one answer per link and line (primary key link and line, so a new answer replaces the old), `correct`, `needs_fix` or `not_sure`, a note of at most 500 characters kept only with `needs_fix`, and the time; it references `public.dialogue_lines (id)`, and a link's answers go when the link goes. Both tables: everything revoked from public, anon, authenticated and service_role, row level security on, no rule.
- **The batches** are worked out by `private.review_batch_dialogues()`, never typed: the published dialogues (an approved dialogue in an approved unit with a stop number, the same rule as `road_for_visitors`), numbered in road order (stop, then the dialogue's place in its stop), 27 to a batch, batch 4 taking the rest. The migrations publish 108 dialogues, 3 per stop on 36 stops, so today each batch has 27: batch 1 is stops 1 to 9, batch 2 stops 10 to 18, batch 3 stops 19 to 27, batch 4 stops 28 to 36. Publishing a new dialogue moves the batches (see "For the owner").
- **The secret.** The Worker makes 32 random bytes (256 bits) with `crypto.getRandomValues` and writes them as 43 base64url characters (`newReviewToken` in `worker/review-links.ts`). It goes only into the email. Every database function that takes it checks its shape and hashes it (`private.review_token_hash`), then looks the hash up, so a copy of the table opens nothing and the stored hash itself does not open the link. The secret is never in an answer to the owner page and never logged; a crash report's path shows it as `:id` (`cleanPath`, tested).
- **The reviewer's routes** (`worker/review-links.ts`, wired in `worker/api.ts`): `GET /api/review/<secret>` (the batch and each dialogue's line and answer counts), `GET /api/review/<secret>/dialogues/<id>` (the dialogue's approved lines with spoken, written, English, the line's two audio files as the same `/audio/` paths the app plays, and this link's saved answer and note) and `POST /api/review/<secret>/answers` (`{lineId, answer, note}`). The Worker has only the publishable key, so it asks as anon through three security definer functions, `review_link_overview`, `review_link_dialogue` and `review_link_answer`. A malformed secret never reaches the database. A wrong, deleted or malformed secret and a dialogue or line outside the batch all get the same `404 {"error":"link_not_found"}`. An answer is checked by the Worker (one of the three, a note of at most 500 characters counted as Postgres counts them, no control character but a line break, a positive whole line id; 400 with the field otherwise; body at most 4 KB, same origin JSON POST only) and again by the database, which also checks the line belongs to the batch. Every answer is `no-store`, `noindex, nofollow` and `no-referrer`; no sign in cookie is read and none is set. `/api/review/answer` stays the learner's daily review.
- **The rate limit** is in the database: at most 60 answers a minute per link, counted on the link's row under a row lock, so parallel calls and several running copies of the Worker cannot beat it. The 61st is `429 limit`, and the page says to wait a minute.
- **The pages** (`src/screens/ReviewLinkPage.tsx`, `src/screens/ReviewLink.module.css`). `/review/<secret>` is the list: the title "Check the Finnish in Aleksi", a short intro, "This is batch 1 of 4, with 27 dialogues in the order a learner meets them.", and one card per dialogue (title, stop, and "Not started", "In progress, 3 of 8 lines" or "Done" as plain text). `/review/<secret>/<dialogue id>` draws each line with the session's own line card (`LineCard`: speaker, "Line 2 of 4", spoken and written Finnish with a Play button each, and the English meaning), then the three answers as the app's buttons (the chosen one is the primary pill and `aria-pressed`). "Needs a fix" opens the feedback page's text box, "What should change?", with "N of 500 characters" and "Save the note". Each save shows "Saved." in the line's status line; a failed save says what happened (no connection, too many at once, link no longer works, try again) and keeps what was typed. A tap while a line is saving is ignored. A wrong or malformed link shows only "This link does not work". Design tokens and existing components only: no new font or colour, no pill labels, no icons before headings.
- **Routing.** `/review/*` is in `PAGE_ROUTES` and `run_worker_first`, so a review page always runs the Worker and is never a public file. `worker/index.ts` answers it with the app shell plus the private headers, drops any `set-cookie` and `etag`, and does not count it as a visit. `PATHS.review` and `isUnder` in `src/lib/app-paths.ts`; `forwardFor` keeps any address under `/review/` (the bare `/review` still goes to the landing page); `src/App.tsx` draws `ReviewLinkPage`.
- **The owner page.** Under each reviewer sign up (`src/screens/OwnerReviewLinks.tsx`, used by `OwnerReviewers.tsx`): one line per link sent ("Batch 2 sent on 24 Nov 2026, 14:30. 0 of 212 lines answered."), a "Batch" choice of 1 to 4 (the first batch not yet sent is offered first), and "Send review link". One press posts `POST /api/owner/review-links/send`; a second press while sending is ignored. Below the sign ups, a new "Review answers" section lists every "Needs a fix" and "Not sure" answer, newest first, with the answer, the reviewer's name, the dialogue, the line number and speaker, the spoken, written and English line, and the note, all as plain text. When the Worker has no `BREVO_API_KEY` the section says "Email sending is not set up on this Worker (BREVO_API_KEY is missing), so no review link can be sent.", and a press gives the same line.
- **The owner routes** (`worker/owner.ts`), behind the same three locks as the rest of the owner host (Cloudflare Access, the Worker's Access token check, the owner's own live sign in checked by the database): `GET /api/owner/review-links` (links, answers and `emailReady`) and `POST /api/owner/review-links/send` (`{signupId, batch}`). The send checks the Brevo setting first (nothing recorded without it), makes the secret, records the link through `owner_create_review_link` (which returns the name and email to send to), and sends the email. If Brevo does not take it, the link is deleted again through `owner_delete_review_link` (502 `not_sent`), so no link exists that nobody received. The link uses `https://<CANONICAL_HOST>/review/<secret>` (meetaleksi.com on the live Worker); without `CANONICAL_HOST` (local runs) the request's own address.
- **The email** (`worker/providers/feedback-alert.ts`, still the only file that knows Brevo). The alerts' sending code is now one private `brevoSend`, shared by the two alerts (unchanged, their tests unchanged and passing) and the new `sendReviewLinkEmail`. From Aleksi <hello@meetaleksi.com>, to the sign up's own email, subject "Your Aleksi review link", body word for word from the spec with the name and link filled in. It needs only `BREVO_API_KEY` (not the owner's inbox) and has no gap, because each send is a press by the owner.
- **Privacy.** `src/privacy/data-inventory.ts`: a second "From people with no account" item, "Review answers" (what, why, consent as the legal reason, "Until the owner deletes the sign-up, which deletes the link and its answers with it."), and one sentence in `HOW_LONG`. `src/privacy/processors.ts`: Brevo's role now also says it sends the review link email and receives the name, email address and that email. Nothing links to an account, so nothing goes into `export_my_data()`.
- **Records.** `docs/conventions.md` has the `[review-links N]` label row; `docs/data-model.md` a "Review links" section.

### For the owner to decide or check

- **The email text says "27 short dialogues"** for every batch, as written. With today's 108 dialogues batch 4 also has 27; if more dialogues are published, batch 4 grows and the email would undercount.
- **Batches move with the content.** A link stores its batch number, not a list of dialogues. Publishing a new dialogue shifts later dialogues into the next batch, and answers already given for a dialogue that moves out of the batch stay saved but are no longer shown on that link's page. They still appear in "Review answers".
- **Replies.** The email comes from hello@meetaleksi.com with no separate reply address; replies reach the owner only if that mailbox forwards to her. The owner's private inbox is never shown to reviewers.
- **"Send no learner cookie".** The review pages set no cookie and the Worker never reads one there. The browser still sends any cookie it already has for meetaleksi.com with the page's requests (same address); the review routes ignore it. Sending none at all would need `credentials: 'omit'`, which Cloudflare Access blocks on the preview links (AGENTS.md lesson).
- **The rate limit** is 60 answers a minute per link, a little over one a second. A reviewer tapping Correct quickly down a long dialogue stays well under it.
- **Found, not changed:** the "Reviewer sign-ups" section from the reviewers job draws an empty orange problem box above the list whenever no Delete has failed (its `StatusLine` is always drawn; see `evidence/1-6-owner-send-and-answers-375x812.png`). It is outside this job; the fix is one line in `SignupList`. Say if you want it.
- **Notice date.** `NOTICE_UPDATED` stays 2026-11-05 (pinned by `src/privacy/first-layer.test.ts`), as in the reviewers job.
