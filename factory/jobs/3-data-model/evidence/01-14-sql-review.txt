Behaviour notes found while reading (not statement failures; listed under "odd" in evaluation.md):
  a) Re-approval can reuse the old approval. Setting an approved row back to draft keeps approved_at and approval_ref. After an edit,
     "update ... set status = 'approved'" alone passes the approval check with the OLD time and reference. Nothing forces a new review
     record for changed content. Job 5 should clear both on un-approve, or the database should require it.
  b) A form can go back to draft while approved lines still link to it. The token stays readable and points at a form the learner
     cannot read. docs/data-model.md says so on purpose (job 6 shows such a word without a card), but the migration comment on
     line_words_have_approved_forms says "a learner never taps a word whose card cannot be shown", which only holds at approval time.
  c) The pgTAP files insert rows into auth.users with only an id. This works on the CI database; whether the hosted postgres role may
     do it is unknown until the merge run (the builder lists it as open).
