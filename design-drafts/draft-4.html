PuhuScribe soft draft

Draft 4 takes the feel of your gym app reference: a soft wash of colour behind every screen, milky white panels with a white edge, light type, lots of space, and one colour for anything you can tap. Welcome, Today and Hear it are shown in lake blue and in birch-forest green.

What was taken from the reference

The wash from one colour into a pale warm sand, as it goes from mint to peach. Milky panels instead of solid white boxes. A welcome headline with one coloured word. Round pill buttons with an arrow in a circle. A ring showing progress, like its 75 percent ring. Small, quiet text. Its photos of people are left out for now, because PuhuScribe has no photos yet.

Your choices after this draft

  1. Lake blue or birch-forest green.
  2. Whether this soft look is the direction for every screen.
  3. Whether you want photos of people in Finland, like the reference uses, or keep the screens without photos.
