# Test 2: the 10 lines for the voice test

Status: draft by Claude, 19 October 2026. Waiting for the native reviewer's check. Not yet used.

## For the reviewer

These 10 short lines will be read aloud by computer voices from 4 companies. You will then rate the voices in a blind listening test (about 1 hour, later this week). Before that, please check the lines themselves (about 15 minutes).

- **Spoken** is everyday spoken Finnish (yleispuhekieli), as a Helsinki adult would say it in 2026. No dialect, no dated slang.
- **Written** is the standard written version of the same line.
- Lines 1 to 5 come from unit 1 (meeting someone at work or a language café). Lines 6 to 10 come from unit 2 (ordering coffee and a pastry).

For each line, please answer in the last column: **OK**, or **Fix** with the corrected wording, plus any comment. Typical questions: would a Helsinki adult really say it this way? Is the spoken spelling how people write it in messages? Is the written version correct?

## Notes for Claude (not for the reviewer)

- The name "Alex" and the country "Brazil" are placeholders, chosen so the line works for any learner. They are not about you.
- Lines 3 and 4 use the most clipped forms (*Helsingis*, *Helsingist*). They are there on purpose: they are the hardest test for a computer voice. The reviewer may prefer *Helsingissä* in a beginner unit.
- After the reviewer's check, each approved line is voiced in both versions by Azure, Google, OpenAI and ElevenLabs, with 2 to 3 voices each.
- These lines are only for the voice test. The real unit 1 and 2 dialogues come later from the content pipeline.
