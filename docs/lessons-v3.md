# PuhuScribe v3.0 lessons

Every lesson planned for version 3.0, in the order a learner meets them. Gathered on 1 November 2026 from the PRD's curriculum (section 6) and the dialogues drafted in `content/drafts/`.

## Where things stand

- **6 units** are planned. Each unit is one real-life situation and ends with an "I can" sentence (the A1 can-do statement).
- **Units 1 to 3** have 2 dialogues each, 6 in total. They are written, checked by the automatic checks, and voiced in spoken and written Finnish. **None is approved yet:** your Finnish reviewer checks all six at the end of the MVP. Until then a learner sees none of them.
- **Units 4 to 6** are planned only. Their dialogues are not written yet (scope item 11). The tutor step with Otso starts at unit 4.
- Each lesson (one session) is one dialogue of about 6 lines: Hear it, Understand it, Say it, Keep it, and from unit 4 Use it.
- The whole version aims at about 180 base words and 300 word forms met in context.

## Unit 1. Meeting someone at work or at a language café

**The learner can:** introduce yourself, say where you are from, and ask the same.

### Lesson 1.1: A new colleague

Maria has just started a new job in Helsinki. In the break room she meets her colleague Aino for the first time. They greet each other, say their names, and ask each other where they are from. Maria is from Brazil. Aino is from Helsinki.

Speakers: Aino, a colleague who has worked there for years; Maria, a newcomer in her first week at the job.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aino | Hei! Mä oon Aino. | Hei! Minä olen Aino. | Hi! I'm Aino. |
| 2 | Maria | Hei! Mä oon Maria. Mä oon uus täällä. | Hei! Minä olen Maria. Minä olen uusi täällä. | Hi! I'm Maria. I'm new here. |
| 3 | Aino | Tervetuloa! Mist sä oot? | Tervetuloa! Mistä sinä olet? | Welcome! Where are you from? |
| 4 | Maria | Mä oon Brasiliasta. Entä sä? | Minä olen Brasiliasta. Entä sinä? | I'm from Brazil. And you? |
| 5 | Aino | Mä oon Helsingist. Kiva tavata, Maria! | Minä olen Helsingistä. Kiva tavata, Maria! | I'm from Helsinki. Nice to meet you, Maria! |
| 6 | Maria | Kiitos! Helsinki on tosi kaunis kaupunki. | Kiitos! Helsinki on tosi kaunis kaupunki. | Thanks! Helsinki is a really beautiful city. |

### Lesson 1.2: At a language café

At a language café in a Helsinki library, Sara, who moved to Finland last month, talks with Leo, a volunteer. They say hello. Leo asks where Sara is from and where she lives now. Sara is from Spain and lives in Espoo. Then Sara asks Leo the same, once with a yes or no question (for example whether he lives in Helsinki). Leo is from Tampere and lives in Helsinki.

Speakers: Leo, a volunteer who chats with newcomers at the language café; Sara, a newcomer who moved to Finland last month.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leo | Hei! Mä oon Leo. Mist sä oot? | Hei! Minä olen Leo. Mistä sinä olet? | Hi! I'm Leo. Where are you from? |
| 2 | Sara | Hei, mä oon Sara. Mä oon Espanjast. | Hei, minä olen Sara. Minä olen Espanjasta. | Hi, I'm Sara. I'm from Spain. |
| 3 | Leo | Kiva! Onks koti nyt Helsingis? | Kiva! Onko koti nyt Helsingissä? | Nice! Is your home in Helsinki now? |
| 4 | Sara | Koti on Espoos. Entä sä? Ootko sä Helsingist? | Koti on Espoossa. Entä sinä? Oletko sinä Helsingistä? | My home is in Espoo. And you? Are you from Helsinki? |
| 5 | Leo | No, Tampere. Mut koti on nyt Helsingis. | Tampere. Mutta koti on nyt Helsingissä. | Well, Tampere. But my home is in Helsinki now. |
| 6 | Sara | Kiva! Tampere on tosi kaunis. | Kiva! Tampere on tosi kaunis. | Nice! Tampere is really beautiful. |

## Unit 2. Ordering coffee and a pastry

**The learner can:** order simple items, ask the price, and say thanks.

### Lesson 2.1: At the café counter

Anna stands at the counter of a busy café in Helsinki. The barista, Emma, greets her. Anna politely asks for a coffee and a cinnamon bun. Emma asks whether she wants oat milk in the coffee, and Anna says yes. Emma hands them over and they both say thanks.

Speakers: Emma, the barista at the counter; Anna, a customer ordering her morning coffee.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emma | Hei! Tervetuloa! | Hei! Tervetuloa! | Hi! Welcome! |
| 2 | Anna | Hei! Yks kahvi ja yks korvapuusti, kiitos. | Hei! Yksi kahvi ja yksi korvapuusti, kiitos. | Hi! One coffee and one cinnamon bun, please. |
| 3 | Emma | Entä kauramaitoo? | Entä kauramaitoa? | And some oat milk? |
| 4 | Anna | Joo, kiitos. | Kyllä, kiitos. | Yes, please. |
| 5 | Emma | Täs on kahvi ja korvapuusti. Kiitti! | Tässä on kahvi ja korvapuusti. Kiitos! | Here's the coffee and the cinnamon bun. Thank you! |
| 6 | Anna | Kiitti, hei! | Kiitos, hei! | Thanks, bye! |

### Lesson 2.2: Asking the price and paying

In the same café, Daniel asks for a tea and a bun. He asks how much it is. The barista, Emma, says the price in whole euros, a number from 1 to 20. Daniel pays and says here you go as he hands over the money. They both say thanks, and Emma wishes him a good day.

Speakers: Emma, the barista at the counter; Daniel, a customer buying a tea and a bun.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daniel | Hei! Saisinks kahvii? | Hei! Saisinko kahvia? | Hi! Could I have some coffee? |
| 2 | Emma | Kyl, tässä. | Kyllä, tässä. | Sure, here you are. |
| 3 | Daniel | Kiitti! Mikä on hinta? | Kiitos! Mikä on hinta? | Thanks! What's the price? |
| 4 | Emma | Se on neljä euroo. | Se on neljä euroa. | It's four euros. |
| 5 | Daniel | Tässä, kiitti! | Tässä, kiitos! | Here you go, thanks! |
| 6 | Emma | Kiitti, hyvää päivää! | Kiitos, hyvää päivää! | Thank you, have a good day! |

## Unit 3. At the grocery store

**The learner can:** ask where something is and understand simple directions.

### Lesson 3.1: Asking where something is

Nina is shopping in a grocery store in Helsinki. She cannot find the oat milk, so she says excuse me to Ville, who works in the store, and asks where the oat milk is. Then she asks whether they have rye bread. Ville answers both questions briefly: the oat milk is over there, and yes, they have rye bread, right here. Nina says thanks.

Speakers: Ville, a staff member stocking the shelves; Nina, a customer looking for two things.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nina | Anteeks, missä on kauramaito? | Anteeksi, missä on kauramaito? | Excuse me, where is the oat milk? |
| 2 | Ville | Se on tuol. | Se on tuolla. | It's over there. |
| 3 | Nina | Kiitti. Onks teil myös kahvia? | Kiitos. Onko teillä myös kahvia? | Thanks. Do you also have coffee? |
| 4 | Ville | On, täs. | On, tässä. | Yes, right here. |
| 5 | Nina | Hyvä, kiitti! | Hyvä, kiitos! | Great, thanks! |
| 6 | Ville | Hei hei! | Hei hei! | Bye! |

### Lesson 3.2: Following simple directions

In the same store, Omar asks Laura, who works there, where the coffee is and where the checkout is. Laura gives short, simple directions: the coffee is over there, on the shelf on the left, and after that Omar should go to the checkout, which is right here. Laura must tell him to go to the checkout in one sentence, using the to-form of the place (as in kassalle, hyllylle), and the checkout itself is right here. Omar checks that he understood, Laura says yes, and they both say thanks.

Speakers: Laura, a staff member at the information desk; Omar, a customer who is new to this store.

| # | Speaker | Spoken Finnish | Written Finnish | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Omar | Hei! Mis on kahvi? Ja mis on kassa? | Hei! Missä on kahvi? Ja missä on kassa? | Hi! Where is the coffee? And where is the checkout? |
| 2 | Laura | Kahvi on tuol, vasemmalla hyllyllä. Sit kassalle. Kassa on täs. | Kahvi on tuolla, vasemmalla hyllyllä. Sitten kassalle. Kassa on tässä. | The coffee is over there, on the shelf on the left. Then to the checkout. The checkout is right here. |
| 3 | Omar | Kahvi on tuol vasemmalla hyllyllä ja kassa on täs? | Kahvi on tuolla vasemmalla hyllyllä ja kassa on tässä? | So the coffee is over there on the left shelf, and the checkout is right here? |
| 4 | Laura | Joo, hyvä! | Kyllä, hyvä! | Yes, that's right! |
| 5 | Omar | Kiitos, hei! | Kiitos, hei! | Thanks, bye! |
| 6 | Laura | Kiitos, hei hei! | Kiitos, hei hei! | Thank you, bye-bye! |

## How this file is made

The unit table comes from the PRD, section 6. The lesson lines come from each `content/drafts/<dialogue>/draft.json`, the newest draft before the native review. When the reviewer approves or fixes a line, the published content in the database is the source of truth, and this file should be regenerated.
